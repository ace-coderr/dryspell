# Dryspell

Parametric weather cover on Arbitrum. A buyer picks a location, a peril (drought or flood) and a payout. Dryspell prices the cover on-chain, locks the premium into an underwriter pool, and pays out automatically when real weather data crosses the trigger. No claims form, no adjuster.

Built for the Arbitrum Open House Dubai Online Buildathon.

Status: under construction. Full documentation lands with the first deployment.

## Engine mode: read this first

The pricing and solvency engine is written twice, with one ABI:

| Engine | Where | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stylus (Rust)** | [contracts/stylus](contracts/stylus) | Complete: 36 tests, builds to WASM. **Not deployed:** Stylus activation was paused on Arbitrum Sepolia, Arbitrum One and the Robinhood Chain testnet when this was built (2026-11-21). |
| **Solidity fallback** | [contracts/solidity/src/DryspellEngineSol.sol](contracts/solidity/src/DryspellEngineSol.sol) | Live fallback with identical selectors, errors and arithmetic, proven equal on 6,672 vectors generated by the Rust code. Reports itself as `solidity-fallback`. |

The app shows which engine is live, read from the chain. Stylus is never claimed unless the deployed engine answers `"stylus"`. See [contracts/solidity/README.md](contracts/solidity/README.md).

## Data sources

Dryspell is priced from real daily rainfall, 1991-2025, for 12 locations across West Africa, East Africa, South Asia and Southeast Asia, from two independent sources: Open-Meteo (ECMWF ERA5, CC BY 4.0, free non-commercial tier) and NASA POWER (cite service and access date). Terms, attribution, method and limits are in [data/README.md](data/README.md).
